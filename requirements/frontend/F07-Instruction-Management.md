# F07 — Instruction Management

**Status:** open

The standing instructions given to every agent, held at two levels — one set for the
machine, one per project — with the rule for how they combine visible rather than
implied.

## Acceptance

- Instructions can be read and edited from the interface at both the machine level and
  the project level.
- A project states, at a glance, which of three positions it is in: carrying no
  instructions of its own, adding to the shared ones, or replacing them.
- Whether a project's instructions add to or replace the shared ones is a single
  reversible choice.
- The fully resolved instructions — exactly what a piece of work would receive — can
  be viewed before starting anything.
- The shipped defaults are readable, and are marked as not editable.

## Notes

The resolved view is the requirement that makes the rest safe. Two levels combined by
a rule are guesswork until somebody can see the result.
