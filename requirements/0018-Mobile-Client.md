# 0018 — Mobile Client

**Status:** open

Scoped deliberately to monitoring and answering. Creating projects, building images
and managing credentials stay where a keyboard is.

## Acceptance

- Fleet overview, task state, decisions and stop all work on a phone.
- The tunnel is held by the application, not configured by hand.
- A dropped connection is obvious and recovers by itself.
- Anything not supported is absent rather than broken.
